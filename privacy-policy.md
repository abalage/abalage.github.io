## Who we are

The website address is: https://balagetech.com.

This is a personal blog operated by Balázs Németh. The site is a **static website** hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), which is operated by GitHub, Inc.

## What personal data we collect and why we collect it

**This website does not collect any personal data on its own.** It is a static site: it has no comment system, no contact forms, no user accounts, no analytics, and it sets no cookies or other tracking technologies. Every page is served to your browser exactly as stored, with no server-side processing of your data.

### GitHub (hosting provider)

Because the site is served through GitHub Pages, GitHub acts as the hosting provider and may collect certain data when you visit the site - for example, your IP address and browser user agent in server logs, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement). This data is collected and processed by GitHub under its own privacy policy, not by this website.

### Embedded content from other websites

Articles on this site may include embedded content (e.g. videos, images, articles, etc.) from third-party services. Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if you had visited the other website directly. Those third parties may collect data about you, use cookies, and monitor your interaction with the embedded content under their own privacy policies.

## Who we share your data with

We do not collect or store any data, so there is nothing to share. Any data collected by GitHub as the hosting provider is handled according to the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## How long we retain your data

We retain nothing, because the site does not collect or store any personal data.

## What rights you have over your data

Since this website does not collect, store, or process any personal data, there is no data we hold about you that could be exported or erased. For data that GitHub may collect as the hosting provider, please refer to the rights described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Where we send your data

We do not send any data anywhere. The site itself has no forms, comments, or tracking, so no data you provide leaves your browser through this website.
